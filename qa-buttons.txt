Unit 1: Photosynthesis
Chlorophyll absorbs light energy, which helps convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.
Photosynthesis stores energy in glucose for later use by the plant.

Unit 2: Cellular respiration
Aerobic respiration releases energy from glucose using oxygen, with most ATP generated by the electron transport chain.
Carbon dioxide and water are produced as waste products in aerobic respiration.

Unit 3: Enzymes
Enzymes lower the activation energy needed for a chemical reaction.
High temperatures can change an enzyme's active site and reduce its activity.
